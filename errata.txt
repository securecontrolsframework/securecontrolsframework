The SCF Council is pleased to announce the release of the latest version of the Secure Controls Framework (SCF). In the past 8+ years, the SCF has expanded from a few dozen laws, regulations and frameworks to over 250. With that magnitude of growth, we felt it was time to perform some de-duplication efforts and reorganize control groupings to make the SCF as efficient and scalable as possible. 

The 34 domains are unchanged. However, most of the SCF's controls have been renumbered in this release. We have made this change as efficient as possible and to minimize disruption, current control # to legacy control # mappings are built directly into the SCF and detailed in the download tabs. The reality is the only constant in life is change and even the SCF has to keep evolving as laws, regulations and frameworks are added to the landscape.

The risk and threat catalogs have been updated, including how they are mapped to SCF controls. Scroll all the way to the right on the SCF spreadsheet and you will find risks and threats mapped to individual controls as Likely, Possible or Unlikely. It is a starting point for your own customization, built to make risk management discussions more efficient.

For detailed changes, please review the ERRATA tab within the SCF spreadsheet or the notes column on the specific Set Theory Relationship Mapping (STRM) document.

You can download the new version of the SCF (XLSX, JSON and other content) from: https://securecontrolsframework.com/free-content/scf-download
