The SCF Council is pleased to announce the release of the latest version of the Secure Controls Framework (SCF). There are two big announcements that are worth noting in this release. The first is the addition of the Quantum Security (QTS) domain to address the threat of Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC). The second is about the Set Theory Relationship Mapping (STRM) project, where all laws, regulations and frameworks now have published STRM (with the exception of NIST 800-53 Rev4 [deprecated] and SIG [Santa Fe Group provides their mappings to the SCF]). The transparency and auditability associated with STRM makes the SCF unrivaled as a control framework for organizations to demonstrate secure, compliant and resilient capabilities.

The SCF currently maps to 252 discrete laws, regulations and frameworks - you can see that complete list on the Focal Documents tab within the SCF, which contains links to the STRM download and the source URL.

Version 2026.2 represents a moderate update, based on:
 New Quantum Security (QTS) domain;
 Updated Set Theory Relationship Mapping (STRM) for multiple laws, regulations and frameworks;
 Updated maturity model criteria;
 Updated “possible solutions & considerations” criteria; and
 Updated compensating controls (see the “SCF Overview & Practitioner Guidebook” for guidance on compensating controls - https://content.securecontrolsframework.com/pdf/scf-recommended-practices.pdf)

You can download the new version of the SCF (XLSX, JSON and other content) from: https://securecontrolsframework.com/free-content/scf-download
